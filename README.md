 ElegantMethods

A curated archive of non-trivial solutions to advanced mathematics and physics problems.

## Overview

This repository contains solutions to challenging problems from multiple sources, including IOQM, JEE Advanced, Olympiad-level contests, and classical problem-solving literature (including Russian and other international problem books).

The focus is not just on obtaining answers, but on developing and documenting efficient, and elegant solution methods that go beyond standard approaches.

## Contents

The repository may include problems from:

- IOQM (Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics)

- JEE Advanced Mathematics and Physics problems

- Physics Olympiads (NSEP level and beyond)

- International Olympiad-style problems

- Classical mathematics and physics problem books 

## Approach

Each solution emphasizes:

- Conceptual clarity

- Alternative or non-obvious methods

- Reduction of computation through insight

## Format

Solutions may be presented as:

- Written PDFs (handwritten or typed)

- Step-by-step derivations

- Structured notes with explanations

## Purpose

This repository serves as a personal archive for deepening problem-solving skills and exploring advanced techniques in mathematics and physics.
